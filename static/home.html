HAWIYA / BIOMETRIC RECOGNITION

How can a device recognize you?

When you unlock a device, it doesn't read your name. It measures the ridges of your fingerprint or the texture of your iris, turns those details into a digital template, and compares each new scan with the saved reference.

Two different patterns. The same basic idea: measure, save, compare. Try both below.

FINGERPRINT

Even the same finger can land differently.

The reader measures where fingerprint ridges end or split. When the finger rotates, those points move. The software aligns the new reading with the saved one before comparing them.

IRIS

The iris is a ring. The computer needs a flat pattern.

The pupil and outer iris mark the ring to read. Software samples those pixels around the circle and lays them out as a rectangle, so the texture can be measured and compared.
